AARP Membership FAQs

Get answers to frequently asked questions about AARP membership.

How old do I have to be to join AARP?

AARP is open to anyone age 50 or older. If you're under 50, you can still join as an associate member.

What do I get when I join AARP?

Membership includes a wide range of benefits, from discounts on travel and dining to advocacy and community service opportunities.

How much does an AARP membership cost?

The standard annual rate is $16, but many new members can join for just $12 or $15 for their first year with automatic renewal.

Does AARP have a loyalty Program?

Yes, AARP Rewards allows you to earn points for learning and taking healthy actions, which can be redeemed for various rewards.

When will I get my AARP membership card?

Digital cards are available immediately through the AARP app, and physical cards typically arrive in the mail within 2-4 weeks.

Is there an App I could use?

Yes, the AARP Now app is available for both iOS and Android devices.
